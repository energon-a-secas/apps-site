# Neorgon Apps: product one-pager

**What it is:** Neorgon's apps for iPhone and Android, each with its help and privacy pages

**Who it is for:** two readers. Someone who has, or is about to install, one of Neorgon's apps and wants to know what it does, where to get it, its help or its privacy policy; and App Review (or Google Play's review), which opens the support and privacy URLs a store record lists before it approves a build.

**Register:** brand (a page someone visits), with document pages under it: the catalog shows each app, and each app's help and privacy pages are plain reading pages with no script.

**The scene:** a phone in one hand, opened from a link in a store listing, an app's About screen or an invite, read for a minute to answer "what is this app, can I get it, and what does it do with my data". Dark like the rest of the fleet; each app's own hue, read from its icon, colours its band in the catalog and its pages, and the mascot icons carry the personality. The per-app hues are a recorded exception to the One-Accent Rule (`docs/DESIGN.md`, Exceptions).

**What done looks like:** every app shows its public status from `data/apps.json` (available, coming soon or an open test, never a build or a review), what it promises, its screenshots with real alt text, one way to get it, and help and privacy pages that answer 200 without a login.
