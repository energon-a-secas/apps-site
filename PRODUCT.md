# Neorgon Apps: product one-pager

**What it is:** Browse Neorgon's iPhone apps, with help and privacy

**Who it is for:** two readers. Someone who has, or is about to install, one of Neorgon's iPhone apps and needs its help or its privacy policy; and App Review, which opens the support and privacy URLs App Store Connect lists before it approves a build.

**Register:** brand (a page someone visits), with document pages under it: the catalog shows each app, and each app's help and privacy pages are plain reading pages with no script.

**The scene:** a phone in one hand, opened from a link in an App Store listing, an app's About screen or a TestFlight invite, read for a minute to answer "what is this app, can I get it, and what does it do with my data". Dark like the rest of the fleet; the screenshots carry the colour.

**What done looks like:** every app's card says its real state (App Store, TestFlight or in development, read from the app's own release docs), shows real screenshots, and links help and privacy pages that answer 200 without a login.
